How File Sharing Works on F Share

Three straightforward steps to send files online between phones, tablets, and computers.

1

Upload Content or Note

Select up to 10 files (photos, videos, PDFs, documents) or type a text message. Choose an expiration window (default 24 hours, or 1h, 6h, 3d, 7d), and optionally set a password.

2

Get Your 6-Character Code

F Share creates a 6-character sharing code and an instant QR code. Senders also receive a private owner deletion token to wipe files anytime.

3

Retrieve & Download

The recipient enters the code on any device to view files, preview documents and videos in their browser, or download everything as an organized ZIP archive.

Why Use F Share for Online File Transfer

Designed for rapid, code-based ephemeral exchanges without unwanted storage footprints.

Zero Registration Required

No accounts, passwords, or email sign-ups. Open F Share, upload your files, share the code, and close the tab. Complete privacy by design.

Bypass Email Attachment Limits

Most email providers reject attachments over 20MB. F Share accommodates up to 50MB per individual file and 100MB per batch with fast direct downloads.

Automatic Permanent Deletion

Files do not sit on servers forever. Our automated background worker permanently removes expired files and database records according to your chosen timer.

Private Server-Side Storage

Files are stored in private cloud storage protected with Row Level Security (RLS). Downloads are gated through authorized backend APIs with signed, short-lived URLs.

Optional Password Protection

Protect sensitive documents with bcrypt password hashing. Files remain encrypted at rest and locked until the recipient authenticates with the correct password.

Owner Deletion Tokens

Need to revoke a transfer immediately? Senders can delete shares on demand at any point before natural expiration using their unique deletion token.
