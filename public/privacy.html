Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

F Share is designed with data minimization as its primary principle. We do not require accounts, usernames, emails, or personal identification. We only temporarily store the files and text content you explicitly choose to upload, accompanied by metadata such as file sizes and selected expiration timestamps.

2. Automatic File Expiration & Deletion

All uploaded files and messages are strictly ephemeral. Once the sender's chosen expiration period arrives (ranging from 30 minutes to 7 days), or when the optional download limit is exhausted, the files are deleted from our storage buckets and database records are removed. Senders can also trigger permanent immediate deletion using their owner deletion token.

3. Storage Security

Uploaded files reside in private cloud storage buckets protected by database Row Level Security. Anonymous users cannot query the directory or inspect bucket listings. Files are only accessible via authenticated API endpoints matching a valid, active sharing code.

4. Rate Limiting & Abuse Prevention

To defend against brute-force attacks and denial-of-service attempts, we maintain temporary, in-memory rate-limiting counters. IP addresses stored for rate-limiting purposes are anonymized using one-way cryptographic SHA-256 hashes and are never sold or shared with third parties.

5. Cookies and Trackers

F Share does not use advertising cookies, third-party behavioral trackers, or analytics pixels.
